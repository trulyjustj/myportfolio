Project 4: Top Spinner

In class, I built a top spinner inspired by Beyblade toys. I first tried making the spinner completely out of gears, but it could not stay upright. After noticing that successful designs used a wheel as the top piece, I changed my design because the wheel moved more mass away from the point of rotation, creating a greater moment of inertia, while its wider body distributed the mass more evenly.

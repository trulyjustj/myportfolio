Project 09: Robotic Arm

Challenge

The challenge is to precisely pick up, move, and place a ping pong ball. The brief highlights stability and repeatability as engineering considerations, along with the order and precision of the robot's movements.

Task Sequence

  • Begin with the device away from the start and end locations.
  • Use a ball placed on the color sensor to trigger movement.
  • Move the robot to retrieve the ball.
  • Move to the end location and place the ball on the end base.
  • Return to the initial position and wait for another ball trigger.

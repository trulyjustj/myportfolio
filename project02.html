Project 2: Silly Walks

The goal of this project was to build a robot that moves forward without using wheels. Because of the limited motor constraint, I chose to place one leg at the front and one at the back on opposite sides to try to get the robot as close to a quadruped. My robot moved forward very slowly and inconsistently; sometimes it moved forward, while other times it stayed in place. In reflection, I noticed that the successful robots built by my peers placed both legs and motors at the front, allowing the robot to drag itself forward rather than walking. They also used rubber feet to grip the carpet more effectively.
